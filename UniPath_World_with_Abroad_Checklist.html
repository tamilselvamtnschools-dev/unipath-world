India admission routes at a glance

The main routes across India.

RouteUsed forHow it works
TNEAB.E./B.Tech in Anna University and most state collegesOnline counselling ranked by Class 12 marks in Maths, Physics and Chemistry. No entrance exam.
NEET-UGMBBS, BDS and other medical coursesClear NEET, then join state counselling (Tamil Nadu Medical Selection Committee) or national counselling.
TNGASAGovernment arts and science collegesOne common online application, with seats allotted by Class 12 marks.
JEE Main / AdvancedIITs, NITs, IIITsRank-based seat allotment through JoSAA/CSAB counselling.
TANCET / CEETA-PGM.E., M.Tech, MBA, MCA at Anna University and affiliated collegesEntrance score decides admission. Some PG seats accept GATE.
CATIIM Tiruchirappalli and other top MBA programmesCAT score, then group discussion/interview.
CUETCentral universities such as Delhi University, BHU, JNUApply for CUET, then the university allots seats by score and its own cut-offs.
CLATNational law universitiesRank in CLAT, then consortium counselling for 5-year LL.B. and LL.M.
University examsVIT (VITEEE), SRM (SRMJEEE), Amrita (AEEE)Each private university runs its own test and counselling.
